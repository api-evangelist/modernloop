# ModernLoop

> ModernLoop is an automated interview scheduling and recruiting-operations platform for talent teams, now paired with "Taylor AI," a configurable AI recruiting agent. It coordinates interviewer calendars, schedules loops across time zones, and integrates with ATS, calendar, video, and coding-assessment tools. ModernLoop consumes partner APIs (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters, Zoom, Google, Slack, Okta) but does not publish a public developer API.
